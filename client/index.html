How to Play

🏦 The Goal

Bankrupt your opponents by buying properties and charging rent. The last player standing wins!

🔥 Auctions

If a player declines to buy a property, it goes to a Free-for-all Auction. Anyone can bid! The highest bidder takes it.

💰 Tax Pot (Free Parking)

All taxes paid go into the central Pot. Land on Tax Heaven to collect the entire pool!

🤝 Trading

At any time, tap a player's card to open the Trade Menu. You can exchange money and properties to complete sets.

🏢 Buildings

Owned a full color set? Tap a property in your info tab to build houses. 4 houses can be upgraded to a Hotel.
